# Shelfwise Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

Shelfwise helps you remember expiry dates. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone, and we do not collect it.

## Your data stays on your device

Items, places, photos, settings and statistics are stored only on your phone. There is no account, and we have no server that receives your data. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes, read dates printed on packaging and take photos of storage places. Text in images is read on your device and photos are never uploaded. You can use the app without granting camera access.

## Product lookup

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number (and nothing else) is sent to the public Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts databases to find the product name. They may see your IP address, as any website does. You can skip scanning and type the details yourself.

## Notifications

Reminders and tips are created on your device. No notification content is sent anywhere. Tips are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

## Backups

A backup is a file you create yourself. It is saved or shared only where you choose. Anyone who receives the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

## Sensitive documents

Please do not store ID, passport, card or account numbers in item names or notes. The app is built for expiry dates, not for secrets.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Deleting your data

Deleting an item or place removes it from the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you created stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app. Questions: shelfwise.support@gmail.com
